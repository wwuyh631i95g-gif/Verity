# BlockWorld AR V2

The V2 build replaces the screen-positioned 2D overlay with ARCore/Sceneform world-space objects.

## Included
- AR camera feed and motion tracking.
- 40 selectable 3D block materials.
- Real anchored blocks: tap a detected real plane to place; tap a block to break it.
- 10 voxel-style 3D creatures, assembled from 3D cube parts.
- Creatures move toward the device in world space and reduce only the in-game health counter when close.
- Health, hunger, XP, inventory, and 9-slot hotbar.
- Friend-name placards anchored to real detected surfaces.

## Important
- The app requires Google Play Services for AR (ARCore) and a compatible/certified device. The Honor X8b was not found in Google's published supported-device list when this build was prepared, so AR tracking may be unavailable on that handset. The app checks support and displays a clear message instead of pretending screen coordinates are real AR.
- Objects remain fixed relative to the room while the AR session is running and tracking is healthy. Closing the app/session clears local anchors. Cross-session or multi-phone shared persistence requires Cloud Anchors and a backend; it is not included.
- Friend labels are local 3D name tags, not real-time multiplayer or automatic friend detection.
- The meshes are original simple voxel-style shapes generated by the app, not imported Minecraft assets.
- Camera permission is required. No location, microphone, contacts, or storage permission is requested.
