# Neon Coast: Last Run 0.3.0

An original compact 3D open-world story and driving game for Android. It aims for some of the broad atmosphere and systems of modern coastal open-world games, while keeping its own fictional city, cast and plot. It does not copy GTA VI code, maps, characters, dialogue, missions or Rockstar assets.

## Story
Kai and Lina investigate a company attempting to take over the Old Port. Five linked story missions use in-game dialogue scenes, a memory-card investigation, a police pursuit and a final public broadcast.

## New in 0.3.0
- Smooth in-game day/night cycle with changing sky, fog, sunlight, lit windows and street lamps.
- Optional rain with animated rain streaks and a darker wet-road look.
- Reworked low-poly sports car: tinted glass, mirrors, bumpers, grille, headlights, brake lights, detailed wheels.
- Improved driving response, collision handling, vehicle condition and fuel readout.
- Safer vehicle entry/exit checks; traffic collisions no longer let the player car pass through other cars.
- Pedestrian spawn spacing and crowd avoidance to reduce characters stacking on top of each other.
- Persistent cash, mission stage, both character positions, selected hero, weather and time.
- GPS route and police markers on the mini-map.
- Responsive HUD spacing, fewer overlapping labels, phone pauses the world and contains weather/car-paint options.

## Existing features
- Fictional coastal 3D city with downtown, residential blocks, beach and marina.
- Two switchable protagonists with separate saved positions.
- Five original story missions and dialogue.
- Police pursuit, drivable car, traffic, pedestrians and touch controls.
- Mini-map, cash display, speedometer and local saves.

## Android
- Package: `com.verity.neoncoast`
- Version: `0.3.0`
- Minimum Android: 8.0 (API 26)
- Target Android: 14 (API 34)
- Landscape orientation; no runtime permissions or network access required.

Three.js is bundled locally into the APK during the GitHub Actions build. No game data is downloaded at launch.
