# Neon Coast: Last Run 0.5.0

An original 3D coastal open-world game for Android. It takes broad inspiration from modern open-world driving adventures, but its map, characters, plot, dialogue, jokes and visual assets are independently created. It is not a GTA VI port and does not reproduce the GTA VI map or Rockstar assets.

## 0.5.0 — Living City & Sunset Pier
- Fixed an accidental duplicate city-generator declaration that caused the smaller legacy 7 x 7 city layout to override the expanded 9 x 15 generator. The larger road grid now has its matching city blocks.
- New original Sunset Pier district with a rotating observation wheel, boardwalk, hotel frontage and night lights.
- Added crosswalks at central intersections, more traffic vehicles and a denser set of walking pedestrians.
- Extended the story from five to eight original missions, including Sunset Pier, Bayline Airport and Cypress Marsh.
- Expanded the collectible hunt from six to ten original animated objects and jokes. Progress is saved locally.
- Added three weather states: clear, rain and coastal fog.
- District name on the HUD now follows the player's location.
- Added three mobile rendering presets: Economy, Balanced and Quality. Balanced is the default.
- Retained the speedway time trial, local save data and existing vehicle systems.

## Existing systems
- Day/night cycle, changing sunlight, sky colour, fog, illuminated windows and street lamps.
- Rain particles and wet-road tint; reduced grip during rain.
- Drivable car with collision response, fuel, vehicle condition, paint and maintenance.
- Two selectable protagonists, original story missions, phone UI, police pursuit and local saves.
- Touch controls, minimap and responsive landscape HUD.

## Android
- Package: `com.verity.neoncoast`
- Version: `0.5.0`
- Minimum Android: 8.0 (API 26)
- Target Android: 14 (API 34)
- Three.js is bundled locally in the APK; no game data downloads at launch.
- Graphics are stylized and procedurally generated for mobile hardware. This build does not contain high-resolution photogrammetry, motion-captured characters, licensed music, a real-world-scale 1:1 city, aircraft or boat driving, interiors for every building, or GTA VI-specific content.

The game uses its own setting, characters and dialogue. It contains no copied GTA VI map, missions, characters, dialogue, music or proprietary assets.
