# Neon Coast: Last Run 0.4.0

An original 3D coastal open-world game for Android. It takes broad inspiration from modern open-world driving adventures, but its map, characters, plot, dialogue and jokes are independently created. It is not a GTA VI port and does not reproduce the GTA VI map or Rockstar assets.

## 0.4.0 — Expanded Coastline & Secrets
- The playable region expands to a roughly 1.4 km square map with a much longer street grid and wider coastal peninsula.
- New original landmarks and districts: Bayline International Airport, Palm Bay Stadium, Bayline Speedway and Cypress Marsh.
- More procedural city blocks with building-overlap checks, textured facade panels and street-level entrances.
- Six original hidden easter eggs: pilot duck, marsh goose, stadium donut, crab blogger, Radio 404 and a tiny UFO. Each has its own joke and a cash reward; collection progress is saved.
- Bayline Speedway time trial with four checkpoints and a 100-second target.
- Expanded traffic and pedestrians, map-wide navigation and new mini-map district labels.
- Shoreline waves keep their spacing rather than bunching together.
- Mini-map shows the larger street network, mission route, police, collectible locations and speedway start.

## Existing systems retained
- Day/night cycle, changing sunlight, sky, fog, illuminated windows and street lamps.
- Optional rain and a wet-road look.
- Detailed stylized car, collision response, fuel, vehicle condition, paint and maintenance.
- Two protagonists, five original story missions, phone UI, police pursuit and local saves.
- Touch controls and responsive HUD designed for mobile screens.

## Android
- Package: `com.verity.neoncoast`
- Version: `0.4.0`
- Minimum Android: 8.0 (API 26)
- Target Android: 14 (API 34)
- Three.js is bundled locally in the APK; no game data downloads at launch.

The game uses optimized stylized 3D graphics for mobile hardware. It contains no copied GTA VI map, missions, characters, dialogue, music or proprietary assets.
